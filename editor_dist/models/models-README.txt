Put your character models here.
================================

On-Set Studio ships without a character. Mixamo's rigs belong to Adobe and
aren't ours to redistribute, so you download your own -- it takes a minute:

  1. Go to https://www.mixamo.com and sign in with a free Adobe account.
  2. Download a rigged character as FBX. The default "X Bot" and "Y Bot"
     figures are the recommended starting point.
  3. Drop the .fbx in THIS folder.

Keep the original filename ("X Bot.fbx" / "Y Bot.fbx") and the editor will
auto-load it on startup. Any Mixamo-rigged FBX works, including your own
characters run through Mixamo's auto-rigger. You can also import a file
directly in the app instead of using this folder.

Until a model is here, the editor boots to an empty stage. That's expected,
not a fault.
